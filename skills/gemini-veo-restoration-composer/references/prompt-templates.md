### Anti-Patterns — real shipped failures; never reproduce these shapes

1. **Anchor scale oscillation** (shipped): the same anchor declared `35 percent` in one IMAGE and `55 percent` in the next with a static camera — the image model re-frames the whole composition every other frame. Scales are copied from the packet verbatim and never change within a shot family; they increase ONLY across the bridge IMAGEs.
2. **Duplicate contradictory anchor stanzas** (shipped): one IMAGE carrying both `Locked anchors: trunk base at Grid C2 (35 percent height), frame at Grid B2 (55 percent height)...` and `Locked anchors: decaying trunk base opening at Grid C2...`. Exactly ONE Locked-anchors sentence per IMAGE, full packet names, no invented extra anchors.
3. **Exterior anchors pinned inside** (shipped): `misty forest canopy at Grid A2` restated in a post-crossing interior frame. After the bridge, the interior anchor set governs; exterior landmarks are behind the camera and must not be re-pinned to Grid cells.
4. **Camera-less bridge frame** (shipped): a threshold beat's settled IMAGE with no camera declaration at all. Every IMAGE opens with its family's static camera sentence.
5. **Impossible camera sweep** (shipped): `Camera pans 5 degrees over 8 seconds` in a static beat, or an unwritten mid-clip pan appearing in a coaxial (non-pan-variant) bridge clip. Bridge clips translate coaxially only, with ONE declared closing pan permitted solely in the pan variant (never a partial/undeclared pan or a second sweep); static beats do not sweep at all (only the final reward may use a gentle sanctioned move).
6. **Horizon indoors** (shipped): `The horizon line remains perfectly level...` stamped on an enclosed interior frame. Indoors always uses `camera pitch locked level; the central vanishing axis stays centered`.
7. **Declared content silently dropped from IMAGE** (shipped, 2026-07-22): a beat's own VIDEO shows a worker carrying in and placing a daybed, side tables, and potted plants — but the paired IMAGE never names any of them, just a generic "everything is now finished" line that borrows nouns from an earlier, unrelated beat (walls/ceiling/floor instead of the actual new furniture). Every concrete new object THIS beat's own VIDEO installs must be named and positioned in its own IMAGE's state-delta sentence — never let it go generic when specific new items exist to describe (see Exemplar C above).

### Threshold Bridge — TBCP Single-Beat Crossing (exterior → interior, v4)

For any beat where the camera physically travels from outside to inside, compose the crossing as ONE beat: `IMAGE T (last exterior) → VIDEO T (the ONLY visible clip, full arc) → IMAGE T+1 (settled interior)`. There is no separate hold/sill/vestibule/turn beat. See `threshold-bridge-consistency-protocol.md`.

IMAGE T+1's own prompt is composed exactly like any other post-crossing frame — use the **Interior IMAGE** exemplars above (Exemplar A is this exact settled/first-reveal frame). Only the VIDEO needs its own template, since it must narrate the *entire* arc — approach, sill crossing, door-frame wipe, settle, and (pan variant only) the closing turn — as one continuous, unbroken shot, anchored normally from IMAGE T to IMAGE T+1 (no redirection).
